# Scam Help & Safety

## Purpose

This page gives Sri Lanka-focused scam awareness help for low-digital-literacy users. It explains what to do before acting on a suspicious message and what to save or report after clicking, sharing details, or losing money.

The page is awareness/help content only. It is not legal, financial, medical, or emergency advice. Users should contact official support, banks, telecom providers, or police channels as appropriate.
